Privacy Policy

🔵

Photo Privacy

We do not store your photos anywhere. All photo processing in Snappy happens exclusively on your device. Your photos never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to download or share them.

When you take or upload photos in our app:

  • All photos are processed entirely within your browser
  • No images are transmitted to any external servers
  • Your photos remain in your temporary browser memory until you close the app
📋

Data Collection

  • Never include any image data or content from your photo sessions
  • Only collect anonymous usage statistics like page views and feature usage
  • Help us improve the application's functionality and user experience
